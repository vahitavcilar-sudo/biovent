## Required next assets

Manufacturer-approved CAD/section/exploded views and additional photographic angles are absent. The interactive workbench uses the genuine render, truthful feature framing, original source-page links and a native-sized surface sample instead. It does not claim to be 3D or a newly rendered engineering illustration. ProMX and Zygoma do not inherit Smart's conical connection or prosthetic compatibility.

Factory photographs, manufacturing-location evidence, regulatory certificates, legal entity/contact confirmation, standalone IFU/surgical manuals and variant-level prosthetic compatibility remain outside the supplied verified evidence. No stock factory/certificate images or invented origin claims fill these gaps. These are content/asset requirements for future publication, not silently completed V12 work.

There is no approved mapped product film in the current production media manifest. V12 presents motion through the genuine still images and JavaScript/CSS. QA recordings from older releases are not product films and are excluded from this release's site and Windows package.

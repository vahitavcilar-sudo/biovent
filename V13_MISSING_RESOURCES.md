# BioVent V13 — technical resource requirements

Available: original English BIOSmart 24-page catalogue and BioVent 18-page portfolio. Their source titles inside the PDFs remain unchanged. The resource center filters actual records and keeps direct open/download links, family associations, page counts, sizes and source language.

Not supplied or not established: standalone prosthetic catalogue; product-specific IFU/surgical protocols; approved CAD/STEP/section views; validated digital CAD library; Ti-Base/scan-body data; component/platform/diameter compatibility matrices; verified quality certificates; approved high-resolution new ProMX original; clean consistent alternate product renders; reviewed product-specific video derivatives and captions/transcripts where informational.

`ResourceCategory` supports implant-catalogue, prosthetic-catalogue, surgical-protocol, ifu, cad-library, quality-document, technical-guide and product-video. Availability is derived from actual document metadata. V13 does not publish fictitious resources, dead download buttons or an empty category filter suggesting files exist.

For each future resource, require owner approval, exact family/model, document version/date, language, intended market/scope, real file, integrity hash, accessible title, pages/size where relevant and original source link. Add catalogue component identifiers only after source-specific review. Adding a general prosthetic PDF must not silently imply ProMX/Zygoma or third-party interchangeability.

## Findings and implementation priorities

- `data/company.ts` leaves legal identity/contact fields null. The V13 user has now approved **Biovent Implant Systems GmbH**, **Am Hagelkreuz 23, 41469 Neuss**. These replace the catalogue's older Bonn-headquarters contact for current company/legal presentation. They do not verify factory location, production processes, registration numbers, certifications or clinical claims. A form recipient, privacy contact and legal review remain absent.
- About repeats broad portfolio messages and a large logo. Replace it with a factual manufacturer profile: approved identity, documented system architecture, evidence-based quality/resource navigation and two professional contact paths. Keep internal slots for future approved manufacturing/certification assets without publishing empty fictional sections.
- The genuine product images differ in viewpoint/light and useful canvas bounds. Improve studio surfaces, family-specific framing and image contrast through layout, not speculative retouching or distorted geometry. Smart's original image remains the master; the additional ProMX original/CAD/section views are unavailable.
- The technical viewer already zooms/pans, but has short summaries, five Smart areas and few source-linked details. Extend verified feature education, include platform-switching/apex where the source supports them, add truthful view context and genuine per-family framing. Never turn image magnification into a measurement or a fake 3D section.
- Prosthetic documentation consists of five Smart category links and two Monoblock components. Build a compact family/category inspector from these genuine records, with source pages, exact references when present and enquiry association. Do not assign compatibility to ProMX/Zygoma or add unverified Ti-Base/scan-body entries.
- Forms have working frontend guards but production destinations are unset. Existing PHP examples use mail acceptance without durable receipt tracking. Build a reusable opt-in PHP receiver compatible with static hosting, strict catalogue validation, same-origin JSON, anti-spam, rate limiting, durable private receipt storage, idempotent retry and a separate delivery worker. Never enable real transmission or claim inbox delivery without configuration and verification.
- Privacy/legal currently describe a preview only. Publish accurate current storage/submission behavior and approved company identity; make endpoint-specific details conditional. Unverified controller contact, retention/legal bases/provider and legal review must remain publication dependencies.
- Homepage uses generic slogans, a dark image studio and initial below-fold media/route prefetch. Re-measure and reduce unnecessary early requests, refine the visible product stage and technical hero copy. Keep V12's established colors and grid.
- V13 explicitly requests focus/interaction pause. This supersedes the V12 no-inspection-pause behavior, while preserving five seconds as the configurable default. Make Play resume reliably even while it owns focus; hover alone need not stop the slider. Recommend longer reading time in configuration documentation rather than silently changing it.
